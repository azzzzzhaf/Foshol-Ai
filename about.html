Our Mission & Vision

Our main goal is to deliver accurate information and smart agricultural solutions to every farmer through technology.

Farmer Empowerment

Accurate crop disease diagnosis right from the field

Through Foshol AI, we want to empower farmers. Using Artificial Intelligence, any farmer can now identify crop problems completely for free just by uploading a picture from their smartphone.

We believe that if farmers get the right information and advice at the right time, crop production can be multiplied. This initiative will help bring financial solvency and security to the agricultural sector worldwide.
